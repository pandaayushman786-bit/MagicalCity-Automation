# Magical City AI YouTube Automation

## Goal
Build a free, beginner-friendly workflow to turn my own stories into YouTube videos.

## Channel
Magical City

## Story
The Crown of Eternity

## Main Character
Elian Ashborne

## Existing Characters
- Princess Elara Veyne
- Other characters will be confirmed after reviewing the existing Episode 1 clips.

## Workflow
1. Accept a story written by the creator.
2. Break the story into scenes.
3. Prepare visual prompts and dialogue.
4. Organize existing video clips.
5. Assemble clips into a complete video.
6. Add narration, music, and captions where possible.
7. Prepare a title, description, tags, and thumbnail.
8. Let the creator review and approve the final video.
9. Upload to YouTube only after approval.

## Requirements
- Budget: ₹0
- Keep the existing AI Reel Maker project untouched.
- Preserve the existing YouTube uploader and OAuth credentials.
- Do not upload videos without the creator's approval.
- Use free tools wherever possible and explain any usage limits.
